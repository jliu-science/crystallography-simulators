Subject: Updates to the Crystallography Simulators

Hi all,

I've updated the crystallography simulators. The main changes:

1. Clearer 3D views: lattice planes are drawn as parallel sheets, the camera is orthographic (no perspective distortion), the view no longer drifts after you let go of the mouse, and labels are bigger and easier to read.
2. More Miller indices: h, k, l now go up to ±4, so you can look at higher-order planes. The reciprocal grid only grows as much as needed, so the screen doesn't fill with points.
3. Better defaults: the Ewald sphere opens with Mo Kα (0.7107 Å) and d = 1.90 Å in a tuned view, and in the Real to Reciprocal simulator the lattice constant a starts at 4 Å (range 3–7 Å).
4. Controls: left button rotates, hold the right button to pan.
5. New title and intro for the site, and a clearer star link: "Found this useful? Star it on GitHub".

Also coming: the Crystallisation Explorer has lighter, cleaner fonts (functionality unchanged).

Link: https://jliu-science.github.io/crystallography-simulators/

If you find it useful, a star on GitHub is much appreciated. Feedback and bug reports are welcome.
