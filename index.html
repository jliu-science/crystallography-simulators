From lattice planes to diffraction

  1. 1 Lattice planes

    A set of parallel (hkl) planes in the crystal, separated by the spacing d.

  2. 2 One reciprocal point

    The whole set of planes becomes a single point G, perpendicular to the planes, at distance 1/d from the origin.

    |G| = 1/d
  3. 3 The Ewald sphere

    A sphere of radius 1/λ through the origin. A beam diffracts when G lies on the sphere, which is Bragg's law.

    2d sin θ = λ
